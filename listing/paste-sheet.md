## App details — limit 500

English:

```
From 27 September 2026, shops selling to consumers in the EU must display the harmonised notice on the two-year legal guarantee. This app adds it to your product pages using the European Commission's own artwork, unaltered, in all 24 official languages. Shoppers see it in the language they are browsing. Gift cards are excluded automatically, and you can exclude further product types or tags. No code, no theme edits, no tracking, no customer data.
```
[450]

responsibility stays with the merchant. Reviewers look for overreach, and the
honest version is also the true one.
